About us

A Fabric Place has been a retail fabric store in Baltimore, Maryland since 1969. Our store is at 6324 Falls Road, Baltimore, MD 21209.

Prices and availability

  • Fabric is sold by the yard and cut to order. Our minimum cut is one quarter yard.
  • Prices and stock can change without notice, and we may limit quantities.
  • Colors can look different on a screen than in person. We are happy to give you a swatch before you buy.

Payment

We accept cash, major credit and debit cards, and A Fabric Place gift cards. Maryland sales tax is added where it applies.

Returns and exchanges

  • Fabric cut to order is final sale, so please check your yardage, color and fiber content before we cut.
  • Unopened buttons, thread, notions and trims in their original packaging may be returned within 14 days with your receipt, for store credit or a refund to your original payment method.
  • If an item is damaged or defective, please call or visit within 7 days and we will make it right.

Gift cards

Gift cards can be used in our store toward fabric, notions and classes. They cannot be exchanged for cash except where the law requires it, and lost or stolen cards cannot be replaced.

Classes and workshops

  • Registration and payment are required to hold your spot.
  • Cancel at least 7 days before the class for a full refund or credit. After that, we may offer credit toward a future class.
  • If we cancel a class or it does not have enough students, you will receive a full refund.
  • Students are asked to bring the supplies on the class list and to be considerate of others in the room.
