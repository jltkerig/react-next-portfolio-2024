Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to see the answer. If you do not find what you need, give us a call.

Visiting Us

What are your store hours?

We are open Monday and Thursday from 10am to 9pm, and Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday from 10am to 5pm. We are closed on Sundays.

Holiday hours can be different, so please call ahead if you are planning a special trip.

Is there parking?

Yes. Customer parking is available at the store, and our team is happy to help you carry your fabric out to the car.

Can someone help me choose fabric?

Of course. Tell us what you are making, and we will help you pick the right fabric, how much to buy and what notions you will need. Bringing your pattern is a big help.

Fabric and Notions

How much fabric can I buy?

Most fabric is sold by the yard and cut to order. The smallest cut we make is a quarter yard, which is about nine inches.

Can I get a fabric swatch?

Yes. Small swatches are free in the store. If you cannot visit, call us and we can mail a few swatches to you.

Should I wash my fabric before sewing?

In most cases, yes. Wash and dry it the same way you plan to care for the finished piece, so it does not shrink later. Silks, wools and some specialty fabrics are best dry-cleaned, so ask us about the fabric you are buying.

Do you carry bridal fabrics and lace?

Yes. We carry bridal satins, French lace, tulle, silks and designer trims. Walk-ins are welcome, and you are also welcome to call and ask when the store is quietest.

Classes

Do you offer sewing classes?

Yes. We hold small classes for beginners, garment fitting and bridal details. New dates are announced on our email list, or you can call the store to ask.

Do I need my own sewing machine?

Beginner classes include a machine to use during class. For other classes, please bring your own machine, and call us if you are not sure what to bring.

Orders and Returns

What is your return policy?

Fabric cut to order is final sale. Unopened buttons, thread, notions and trims can be returned within 14 days with your receipt. You can read all the details in our Terms & Conditions.

Do you ship?

Yes. We can ship cut fabric and notions within the United States. Call us with what you need, and we will quote the shipping before anything is sent.

Do you sell gift cards?

We do. Gift cards are available in any amount at the store, and they make a great gift for anyone who sews.

How do I join your email list?

Use the sign-up form near the bottom of our home page. You will hear from us a few times a month, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

How can I contact you?

Call us at 410-828-6777 or stop by 6324 Falls Road in Baltimore. We would love to hear from you.
