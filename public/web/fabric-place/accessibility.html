For Everyone

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use our website comfortably.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Our commitment

A Fabric Place aims to make this website easy to use for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or other assistive technology. We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

What we have done

  • Large, easy-to-read text with strong color contrast.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a clear outline showing where you are.
  • A “Skip to content” link at the top of every page.
  • Descriptive text for photos, and labels on every form field.
  • A button to pause the slideshow on the home page. The slideshow does not play automatically if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Pages that work on phones, tablets and computers, and that can be zoomed in to 200%.

Known limits

The map on our website is provided by Google, and we cannot control how accessible it is. Our address, hours and a link for directions are always written out in plain text next to it.

Tell us if something is hard to use

If you have trouble with any part of this website, or you need information in another way, please call us or stop by the store. We will be glad to help and will work to fix the problem.
