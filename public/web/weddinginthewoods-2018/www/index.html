Directions

Camp Hidden Valley is located in the remote area of White Hall Maryland.

Please take care when driving to the Camp once you leave the main roads. The area is filled with farms and it isn't uncommon for the farmers to drive their farm equipment on the roads. Since the roads are hilly and windy, there are many blind corners. We highly discourage speeding. Please slow down and enjoy the scenery.

The road into the Camp is shared with the property owners. Upon entering, the road will fork. Stay to the right following the camp signs and the road will lead to a paved parking area between buildings. Please park and follow the signs to the ceremony area.

Accommodations

Guests are welcome to camp overnight.

For those who enjoy camping, we will have ample space to pitch a tent. The camp has several barrack style cabins which house bunk beds for those who still enjoy a roof over their head.

If you're planning to stay overnight, there is a small fee of $15 per person. Money may be sent through Paypal/Venmo/Kelle before the event to reserve your spot. If you aren't sure, don't worry: you can pay the day of!

We will be providing breakfast to those who stay overnight at the camp.
