Answers to Common Questions Asked By Potential Alliance Partners

Q: I already have trouble keeping up with the subscriptions they already have. How on earth do you expect me to keep up with everything Stansberry Research publishes?

We hear this a lot. You've got family... a job... kids or grand kids and you don't want to spend every waking hour paging through financial research. That's why we do something special for our Alliance members. We publish a weekly Executive Summary that gives you a bird's-eye view of what's going on in our business. In the space of 5 minutes - you can get up to speed on all our recent recommendations. Then you can decide what you'd like to focus on. It's one of our most popular features. It can literally save you hours every week by letting you skip any ideas you aren't interested in. As an Alliance member you'll receive your Executive Summary each Monday.

Q: I'm only using one specific investment specific strategy? Wouldn't I be better off just subscribing to the individual products?

No. It doesn't matter which investing style you prefer. You could use aggressive, quick paced trades. You could be interested in buy-and-hold, income generating recommendations. Or you might use a blended strategy. No matter what your investing style is we have a handful of advisory letters that will help you get to where you want to go.

When you sign up for services one at a time, it can get expensive fast. Let's say you're an options trader. We currently have 4 different options trading products that retail for $13,500 a year altogether.

That's basically the entire Alliance entrance fee all by itself. And of course, you'd be paying thousands of dollars in renewal fees every year.

As an Alliance member you'll never pay those renewal fees ever again. That saves you thousands by year 2. Plus when you join the Alliance you get virtually everything else we publish — for life. So if the market changes... Or you just want to experiment with another investment style... You'll still have everything you need. And it won't cost you an extra penny.

You can think of the Alliance as a well-stocked toolbox for your portfolio. You won't need to use every tool at any given time, but you'll always have the tool you need no matter what happens.

Another big thing is the time factor. When you become an Alliance member there's no pressure to dive into a strategy you're interested in but not quite comfortable with.

For example: If you pay $4,000 for our Short Report, you might feel pressure to start taking positions immediately. After all you only have a year and the clock is ticking. And that's how investors make mistakes and lose money.

The best strategy is to take some time and read up on all our services. Become familiar with how everything works before jumping in with your hard-earned money. As an Alliance member you have the freedom to do that.

Q: What does "lifetime membership" mean? Whose lifetime are we talking about?

As an Alliance member you'll get everything we publish — except for Stansberry Venture — for as long as we remain in business. You can even pass your Alliance membership onto your heirs. In fact, we've heard from dozens of members who've included their membership in their will... To make sure a son or daughter has lifetime access to our research.

Q: What happens if the business goes under or something happens to Porter?

Stansberry Research isn't going anywhere. We're on track for record breaking growth this year. And we intend to keep publishing financial research for decades.

Also you need to keep in mind that Stansberry Research is so much more than the handful of people you hear from on a regular basis. We currently have over 170 employees spread out over a couple of different buildings. If one of our analysts decides to leave... Or — God forbid — Porter gets hit by a bus... The rest of us would still be here, ready to provide you with the world class research you've grown accustomed to.

Q: I already have a financial advisor. Why should I join the Alliance?

A good financial advisor is worth their weight in gold. If they can perform their own due diligence, analysis, provide tax advice, assist with goal setting, keep costs low, and stay in contact with their clients then they're invaluable.

Unfortunately most advisors put their clients in cookie cutter asset allocation models. Models filled with annuities and expensive mutual funds. Returns get eaten up by management fees and most people don't realize the damage being done until it's too late.

A lot of our subscribers have financial advisors and use our research to keep them honest. Having access to our research and insight will help you build your knowledge of finance. You'll stay up-to-date on market and economic trends.

That will let you have better conversations with your advisor, because you'll be able to speak the same language. Think about it this way:

Two people walk into a car dealership to shop for a new car. The first one doesn't know anything about cars. The second person did their homework and understands pricing, features, add-ons, and everything else. Who do you think will get better service and a better deal? You want to be the guy who knows his stuff.

It happened again in 2008, we launched Put Strategy Report, which took advantage of a unique anomaly in the options market. Again — Alliance members were the only ones to access this research from the start. And it turned out to be one of the most successful strategies we ever shared with our readers. Over the next couple years, subscribers to Put Strategy Report saw 15 different positions gain 100% or more.

And in the last couple years alone, we launched three new letters... Paul Mampilly's Professional Speculator... Doc Eifrig's Income Intelligence... plus Stansberry International. Again — this opportunity to beta test our new research was ONLY available to Alliance members.

The portfolio published four times a year, and it's basically the 16 best recommendations from among all the work we do. The recommendations are broken down into four categories: 4 growth stocks... 4 value stocks... 4 income securities... and 4 "macro" recommendations. The whole point of this report is to make it easy for you to put our best research to work.

These things are a blast to attend. Alliance members get to connect with other members of the group; have some fun eating good food, have a few drinks, relax, maybe playing some golf... and of course discuss the best current moneymaking opportunities around the world, which you get to hear about before anyone else does.

A lot of new Alliance members are in their 70s or older. None decided they were too old to join. With all the advances in health care today 70 is the new 55. Many of them could be using their subscriptions for the next 30 years.

And when they're done with it in 40 years it's a life changing gift they can leave their heirs. Joining the Alliance means investing in your family's future. You guarantee that your loved ones will have lifetime access to actionable financial recommendations. And some of the best educational resources in the world.

And the great part is, Uncle Sam can't touch it. If you were to will $15,000 to your grandson, then Uncle Sam would step in and take his $6,000 or $7,000. With the Alliance, you hand down your membership to the next generation tax free.

Q: Do I need to have a large amount of investable wealth to become an Alliance member?

We've heard from a number of members who initially lost sleep over their Alliance Partnership. At the time, it seemed like a lot of money to spend on financial research. But we've heard from a lot of members who were thankful they made the decision to become a lifelong learner - before they had much money to invest. Obviously, you shouldn't become a member if the fee would put you in a financial bind. Only you can make the decision whether now is the right time. If you see investing as a life-long pursuit, odds are, you'll enjoy being an Alliance member.

You're getting a professional team to do all the heavy lifting on your portfolio for the rest of your life. You won't have to worry about analysis, due diligence, finding the right stock or right entrance price if you don't want to. All you have to do is decide what idea is right for you and buy the stock. Compared to the cost of getting that kind of analysis anywhere else I think the Alliance is one of the best investments someone can make.

And if you don't want to lay out the entire entrance fee today that's ok. We can split it into quarterly installments, spreading out your payments over the next year. It doesn't cost anything extra to go that route – we just do it because it can help our subscribers out from a cash flow perspective.
